How it works

From invoice to writeback, automatically

ShopConnect sits between your merchant's software and their payment processor, so every payment lands in both places.

01

Invoice scraped

The ShopConnect extension reads the open invoice or work order straight from the merchant's software.

02

Payment started

The amount is sent to the merchant's processor — card-present on a terminal or card-not-present online.

03

Card presented

The customer taps, dips, or pays a link. Approvals come back in real time with full transaction detail.

04

Synced to software

The payment writes back into the invoice automatically. Books stay balanced with zero double entry.

Platform

Everything a payment operation needs

One console for merchants, transactions, terminals, and the sync pipeline behind them.

Card Vault

Card-on-file tokenization through your processor's vault. ShopConnect never touches raw card numbers — only vault tokens and the last four.

Recurring Billing

Create subscriptions linked to vaulted cards. ShopConnect charges automatically on schedule and tracks MRR, past-due, and retries.

Terminals

Register and monitor countertop terminals across every location — online status, validation, and signature capture settings in one place.

Sync Status & Writebacks

Debug merchant issues with raw payloads and one-click retries. Every writeback is tracked from queued to complete.

Analytics & Reports

Approval rates, card brand mix, decline reasons, and month-over-month volume. Download transaction and merchant data as CSV.

Reconciliation & Disputes

Daily batches, settlement records, and deposit tracking — plus a full dispute workflow with evidence submission.
