FAQ

Questions, answered.

The full list. If yours isn't here, email support@shopconnectpro.com and a human will answer it.

What is ShopConnect Pro?

A browser extension and server-side bridge that connects the business software you already run to a lower-cost payment processor. We don’t replace your software or rebuild your checkout - we sit between the two, route payments through better rails, and reconcile the result to the originating invoice automatically.

How fast can I go live?

Typically 48 hours from your first call: rate quote, underwriting with the processor you choose, extension install, and a test payment reconciled end to end. Complex multi-location setups can take a few days longer.

Do I have to change how my team works?

No. Your team keeps working inside the same software - same invoices, same screens. The extension adds one panel for taking payment; everything else, including marking the invoice paid, happens automatically.

Which processors do you support?

Eleven and counting: Stripe, Valor, Windcave, Dejavoo SPIn, Dejavoo iPOSpays, EPX Terminal, EPX Gateway, Maverick, North, Authorize.net, and NMI. We quote across them and recommend the best fit for your volume and card mix.

Is my customers' card data safe?

Card numbers never touch ShopConnect Pro. Payments are tokenized in the processor’s PCI-compliant vault; the bridge only ever handles the vault token and the last four digits. Card-on-file and recurring billing run on the same tokens.

How does surcharging / cash discounting work?

Surcharging adds a disclosed fee on credit transactions only (debit and prepaid exempt, capped at cost and never above 3%, registered with the networks 30 days ahead). Cash discounting builds card cost into list price and discounts cash at checkout - simpler rules, legal in all 50 states. We build the disclosures, file the registrations, and run the math so refunds and debit behave correctly. Done right, your net cost of card acceptance approaches zero.

How does auto reconciliation work?

The bridge stamps every charge with the originating invoice ID, posts it the moment funding clears, and handles partial payments, surcharges, tips, and refunds without breaking the link. On Tier 1 platforms this is a dedicated integration; on Tier 2 the universal bridge delivers the same outcome. Invoices simply mark themselves paid.

What happens to refunds and disputes?

Refunds - full or partial - flow back through the bridge and post against the original invoice. Disputes surface in your ShopConnect console with the evidence workflow built in, so you respond once, in one place.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term contracts, no early termination fees. Uninstall the extension and your software works exactly as it did before us.

What does it cost?

The extension is free. Your processing rate is custom-quoted from your actual statement - typically 30–50% below a software-bundled rate on standard pricing, or near-zero net cost on dual pricing. See pricing.

My software isn't on your list. Am I stuck?

Probably not - the universal bridge can connect to any web-based system. Tell us what you run and we’ll move it to the front of the integration queue.
