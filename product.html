Product

One extension between your software
and better payment rails.

ShopConnect Pro doesn't replace anything. It reads the invoice that's already on your screen, runs the payment through your processor, and writes the result back where it belongs.

From invoice to writeback, automatically.

Four steps, every time, on every supported platform.

01

Invoice scraped

The extension reads the open invoice or work order straight from the merchant's software - amount, customer, invoice number.

02

Payment started

The amount is sent to your processor: card-present on a terminal, or card-not-present by text, email, or link.

03

Card presented

The customer taps, dips, or pays the link. Approvals come back in real time with full transaction detail.

04

Synced to software

The payment writes back into the originating invoice automatically. No double entry, ever.

Every way your customers pay.

One bridge, seven payment channels - all reconciled against the originating invoice.

Terminal

Tap, dip, or swipe on a countertop terminal. Card-present rates, EMV, tips, and signature capture.

Invoice

Charge the open invoice directly from your software. The amount is read for you; no re-typing.

Text-to-Pay

Send a secure payment link by SMS. The customer pays on their phone; the invoice reconciles itself.

Keyed

Card-not-present entry for phone orders, with address verification handled correctly.

Cash

Record cash payments through the same flow so your software's books stay complete.

Refund & Deposit

Full and partial refunds and deposits flow back through the bridge and post against the original invoice.

Security

Card numbers never touch ShopConnect Pro. Payments are tokenized in your processor's PCI-compliant vault; we only ever see the vault token and the last four digits. Surcharge and cash-discount programs run with the disclosures, caps, and card-brand registrations handled for you.
