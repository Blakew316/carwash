What we believe.

Your software should compete for your business every month, and so should your processor. When the two are bundled, neither has to. The bundled processor's markup - we call it The Software Tax - survives because switching away means re-platforming your whole operation.

So we removed the re-platforming. ShopConnect Pro is a bridge, not a replacement: your team keeps the software they know, and your payments move to rails that were bid for, not bundled in.
