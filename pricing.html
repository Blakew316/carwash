Dual pricing

Net cost of cards: close to zero.

Pass processing costs through to the customers who choose to pay by card - via compliant surcharging or cash discounting. Legal in all 50 states when done correctly, and we do it correctly.

Pricing questions, answered.

Is the extension really free?

Yes. There's no license fee, no per-seat charge, and no setup cost. We earn on the processing relationship - which is also why it's in our interest that your rate stays low and your volume stays healthy.

Are there contracts or termination fees?

No long-term contracts and no early termination fees. If we stop earning the business, you leave. Your software keeps working exactly as it did before you installed us.

What affects my quoted rate?

Monthly volume, average ticket size, card mix (debit vs. rewards credit vs. Amex), refund rate, and whether transactions are card-present or card-not-present. That's why we quote from your actual statement instead of publishing a one-size-fits-none number.
